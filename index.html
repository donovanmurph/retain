    How Retain helps

    Spot the signs before the summer scramble starts.

    Flags teachers at risk of leaving

    Retain reads each teacher's file and points out signs they may leave, such as a new teacher with no mentor or a pending transfer request.

    Explains every flag

    Each flag lists the facts that caused it and the file they came from, so you know what to address and can check it yourself.

    Keeps candidates organized

    Prospective hires sit in one place with their own flags, like a license that expires before the start date, so you can fill open positions faster.

    Our approach

    Retain reads and flags. The decisions stay with you.

    It never contacts anyone

    No emails, calls or messages go to teachers or candidates. You decide who to talk to.

    It doesn't make assumptions

    A flag is a sign worth checking, not a judgment about a person.

    It only uses what's in your files

    Every fact comes from your district's records. If something is missing, Retain asks you instead of guessing.

    It sticks to hiring and retention

    Payroll, benefits and discipline stay in the systems you already use.
